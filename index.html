  1. 2026 - 2 Hour Narrative Game Jam

    Homebrew Antiques

    The project

    Homebrew Antiques is a whimsical narrative adventure made for a 2-hour game jam. You wake up as a goblin, cursed by a witch Karen, and have to run your business with your new goblin mindset. Your choices shape the story, and some of them get people killed, but at least you end up a happy goblin.

    My role

    I was the programmer and the manager of the team. As manager, I split the work into tasks that fit the strengths of each team member and made sure that the scope was doable in a 2 hours period. As programmer, I built the game in Ren'Py. I combined everyone's art, writing into one working game. The finished game is published on itch.io After the jam, I released version 1.2 with small code fixes, and the code is public on GitHub.

    Tools used

    • Ren'py
